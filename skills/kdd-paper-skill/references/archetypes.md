# KDD contribution archetypes

Choose evidence for the contribution the author actually makes.

- **Method or algorithm:** identify the changed operation or representation,
  the intended role, and evidence for the claimed difference. Do not require a
  theorem, a new dataset, or an isolated benefit from every component.
- **Theory:** state objects, assumptions, quantifiers, guarantees, and the
  exact comparison. Separate sufficient from necessary conditions and upper
  from matching bounds.
- **Empirical finding:** describe the observation, protocol, conditions, and
  uncertainty. Do not turn a descriptive result into a causal claim.
- **System or efficiency:** state interfaces, workload, resource boundary,
  and whether the comparison is end-to-end or component-level.
- **Dataset, benchmark, or resource:** state units, construction, coverage,
  access, splits, licenses, and activities enabled. Intended and demonstrated
  uses differ.
- **Applied or deployment work:** state the operating context, constraints,
  monitoring or decision boundary, and what was actually deployed.
- **Position or visionary work:** separate thesis, supporting argument,
  evidence, counterposition, transfer limit, and proposed action.

These are routing choices, not admission tests. A paper may have more than one
coherent contribution.
